## Versioning

NicoRed follows semantic versioning where applicable:

- **MAJOR** – Major architectural changes or incompatible changes.
- **MINOR** – New functionality and significant improvements.
- **PATCH** – Bug fixes and smaller improvements.

Pre-release versions may use suffixes such as:

- `alpha` – Early development.
- `beta` – Feature testing.
- `rc` – Release candidate.
